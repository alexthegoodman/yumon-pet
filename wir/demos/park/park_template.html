Yumon Park

Thirty species built from permutations of capabilities, hunting and hiding across four bands. Every Yumon carries its own copy of one compiled WIR brain. It starts as the written rule, learns from its own life while it sleeps, and passes what it learned on to its children through its mother.

Populations

Capabilities

Each capability is carried by a limb, and the limb's size gene sets how well it works. Claws carry both climbing and clawing, so one gene moves both. Every limb costs upkeep, and wings must lift the body: a lineage that grows too heavy for its wings stops flying.

How a life works

Each tick a Yumon senses the nearest threat, food, prey and mate as distance buckets, feeds them to its brain with its hunger, fatigue and hurt, and samples one of eight options from a softmax over the brain's keys. Its reward is how much the tick reduced its need (hunger, fatigue and hurt, squared), plus a bonus for mating and for giving birth.

When it falls asleep it takes one policy-gradient step on its own weights over what happened since the last sleep, held near the weights it was born with. A child gets a blend of both parents' genes with one small mutation, and a copy of its mother's brain. When a Yumon dies its brain leaves the cache.
