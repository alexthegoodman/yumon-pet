# Yumon v0.2.0

Yumon Portal v0.2.0 is deliberately small: chat directly with Yumon, and watch the WIR Yumon Ant colony and WIR Chess player, whose judgment comes from written neural networks.

However, this was only the first stage to Yumon. Yumon is envisioned as **a companion who entertains and socializes to the benefit of his owner**. He is not utility focused, and thus does not require broad knowledge or problem solving.

He is designed to be extremely lightweight, as has been the case thus far. Therefore, he will retain his system of independent models, while improving upon it substantially and making it more cohesive.

Rather than achieve a friendly, entertaining companion (for now, only in digital worlds) via a complex, large language model, we will achieve it through the harmonious orchestration of various small models.

We can leverage RoPE and M-RoPE and modify it as necessary to support richer world understanding in any single model.

We will leverage Kilo Talk, our compressed form of English, in order to allow for our small embedding dimensions.

We will leverage LSTM, number-mapped, or classification models as needed, only calling for GAN (images) or MoE (text+) in the preferred scenarios.

We will continue to leverage freely available datasets on Kaggle and Huggingface, while also creating our own synthetic data with the Qwen models on Huggingface Inference Endpoints.

Synethetic data is our choice solution, but can be seeded with handcrafted or real data.
