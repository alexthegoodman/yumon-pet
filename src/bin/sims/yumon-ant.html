How a colony works

Five distance fields run with the compiled path step over every cell an ant can stand on: to the nest chamber, to food one ant can lift, to the dig face, to the dump on the hill, to heavy items short of ants. Each ant's brain turns hunger, age, its role (digger, forager or helper) and the colony's store into drives, follows the strongest one's field, and sets action bits. Heavy food and pebbles move only when enough ants grip them. Dug sand is carried out and dropped beside the entrance, which is how the hill grows. The queen lays eggs while there is food to spare and room underground; when the colony fills, it plans new tunnels and chambers.

Hover the glass to read a cell. Sand you pour slides like sand; if it buries the entrance, the diggers clear it.
